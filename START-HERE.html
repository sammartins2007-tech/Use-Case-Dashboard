Good to know

Questions people ask

Where does my data live?

Only in your own SharePoint site and your own Copilot Studio environment. The kit itself never holds customer data.

Can I share it with my account team or the customer?

Yes, as optional extras: a read-only app per account team, and a customer-safe edition with an offline copy for guests. Nothing is shared without your approval.

What does it cost to run?

The app shows an estimate of every AI run (refresh, coach, reviews). The eight SE agent calls use Copilot Credits in your environment. Actual billing is in the Microsoft 365 admin centre.

Why does the first refresh wait for me?

The first runs may ask a one-time approval for the tools they use. Approve once and the refresh runs on its own afterwards.

Does it invent anything?

No. A missing fact stays empty or marked "to confirm", and contradictions become red questions for you to settle in the app.
